# Campaign Strategy — Mission Chain

Turn an Owner goal into a concrete, product-aligned campaign brief + calendar.

## Method
1. **Pin the goal → KPI.** Current posture is **Trust & Discovery** (credibility+SEED, listings+awareness, community retention). Pick a measurable KPI per goal (reach, new community members, sentiment, listing-readiness %). Avoid conversion CTAs until PreSale launches.
2. **Segment the audience:** (a) faith/Christian community, (b) Web3 investors/crypto-natives, (c) regional EN/VI/ES/PT/KO.
3. **Choose content pillars & weight them to the goal.** Current weights: On-chain transparency (high) · Education (high) · Faith & Mission (medium) · Community/testimonial (medium) · Milestone/product update (event-driven).
4. **Map each asset to a milestone.** SEED is live (totalSold currently 0) → lead with proof + education, not urgency. PreSale/MICE later → referral & growth. Listings pending → PR kit + active-channel proof.
5. **Build the calendar** at a cadence the compliance gate can sustain. Note source (original vs repurposed) — one YouTube episode → 4–6 TikTok/Shorts cuts + X clips.

## Output
`_workspace/A_strategist_campaign-brief.md` (goal, KPI, segments, pillar weights) + `_workspace/A_strategist_calendar.md` (weekly rows: date, channel, pillar, goal, asset-type, source, owner-agent).

## Principles
- Proof over hype — MC's edge is 21 BSCScan-verified contracts.
- One asset = one message = one pillar = one goal.
- A missed post beats a missed compliance gate.
